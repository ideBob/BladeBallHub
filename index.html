Powerful Features

Isolated systems, event-driven updates, and a WindUI window that stays responsive on desktop and mobile executors.

Infinite Jump

Repeated jumping functionality with responsive character handling.

No Fog

Improves visibility by removing excessive environmental fog.

Full Bright

Provides a brighter environment for improved visibility.

Remove Jitter

Designed to reduce unnecessary visual jitter.

ESP

Provides additional visual information about relevant objects and players.

Auto Parry

Automatically analyzes the incoming ball and determines an appropriate parry timing.

Auto Dash

Automatically reacts to dangerous incoming situations with configurable movement behavior.

Ball Speed Tracker

Displays useful ball information such as speed, distance, and approach state.

Customize Your Hub

Choose the visual style that fits your setup.
